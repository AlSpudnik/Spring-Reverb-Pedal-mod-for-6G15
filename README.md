The pedal attachment can be used with the Fender Reverb Unit and all its clones.

 <b>TDA2030</b>

Current control: The magnetic field that drives the springs depends solely on the current flowing through the coil, not on the voltage. If a signal is fed to the coil directly from the output of a standard voltage amplifier (TDA2030 module), the high frequencies will be severely attenuated, resulting in a dull, "boomy" reverberation.

To preserve the brightness of the reverberation, it is necessary to limit the current at low frequencies and equalize it at high frequencies. To achieve this, a current-limiting resistor, R29, is placed between the module output and the reverberator. When operating at a 12V supply voltage, it is advisable to use a resistor with a power rating of at least 1 watt.
